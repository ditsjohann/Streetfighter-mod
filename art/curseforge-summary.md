# CurseForge summary

**Short summary (≤ 150 chars):**
Hold E on any mob to fight it in a cinematic 2D fighting-game battle right in your world. Win to learn its style, then finish it with a Fatality.

**Description intro:**
Hostile mobs stop attacking you. To settle things, look at any mob and hold **E**: the camera swings out into a side
view and you fight a real 2D fighting-game battle on the actual terrain. Beat a mob and you learn its fighting style.

- 21 playable styles (Steve, Pig, Skeleton, Villager, Chicken, Goat, Slime, Spider and more), each with specials and a super
- Cinematic camera, limb-by-limb animations and real Minecraft effects and projectiles
- Character select screen, Ki Clash, X-ray finishers and a unique Fatality for every style
- Built-in Fight Guide (press **G**) and a step-by-step first-fight tutorial

Forge 1.21.1.
